YOU DESERVE REAL SUPPORT

Are you in immediate danger?

If you might hurt yourself or someone else, or cannot keep yourself safe, please don't rely on this app.

  1. Contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.
  2. Tell someone you trust what is happening and ask them to stay with you.
  3. Move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself, if you can do so safely.

If this isn't immediate danger but things feel too heavy, contact a qualified mental health professional, school counsellor, or trusted adult today.

MindMate does not monitor messages or contact emergency services.
